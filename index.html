A birthday letter

Meeting you was written in the stars and sometimes I still find it hard to believe that something so beautiful began with such a simple question. It feels like yesterday when you walked up to me and asked, “Are you going for tutorial?” Little did I know that one simple question would be the beginning of a beautiful journey, a friendship and a bond that has become so special to me ❤️🥹

Thank you for being my friend, for being someone I can share my laughter with, for making ordinary days feel extraordinary and for bringing so much light into my life without even trying. Thank you for the memories we’ve made, the little moments that mean more than words can explain and for being the beautiful soul that you are

I know I promised to make your day special and I wish so badly that I could be right there beside you to celebrate you, hug you tightly, make you laugh and remind you in person of just how much you mean to me. But trust me, me not being physically present with you doesn't make today any less special, because you deserve to be celebrated in every possible way. So please, eat to your heart’s content, dress up beautifully, take all the pictures, party, laugh, dance and enjoy your day to the fullest my love. Don't let anything steal your happiness today. You deserve every beautiful thing this life has to offer❤️🎂🥂

And beyond today, I pray that life gives you a thousand reasons to smile. May you never have to beg for the love you freely give to others. May your heart always find peace, may your dreams become your reality and may you walk into rooms where your presence is appreciated, your efforts are recognised and your heart is treated with the tenderness it deserves. May this new chapter bring you unexpected blessings, beautiful opportunities, genuine happiness and the kind of love that makes you feel safe, valued and cherished

I hope you never forget how special you are, not just to me but to everyone lucky enough to know you. You have a beautiful way of making life sweeter and I hope life returns that sweetness to you in countless ways. You deserve happiness that doesn't fade when the celebrations are over, love that shows up even on your difficult days and friendships that remain beautiful through every season of life

Thank you for allowing me to be part of your world. Thank you for every moment, every laugh, every conversation and every memory that has made this journey worth it. If I could go back to that day when you walked up to me and asked if I was going to tutorial, I would choose that moment all over again, because it led me to YOU and that is one of the most beautiful things life has given me🥹❤️

Happy birthday to my beautiful girl, my love, my friend and one of the sweetest gifts life has given me. May this new age be softer, brighter, kinder and more beautiful than you ever imagined. May you flourish in everything you do and may your smile never lose its light.

I love you more than these words can fully express and I'm so grateful that our paths crossed. Here's to you, to your beautiful life, to more memories, more laughter, more adventures and to a friendship I will always treasure. Happy birthday my love. Go and shine like the star you are 🥹❤️🎉💕
